# PetFam

**English** | [한국어](README.ko.md)

**Your pet family, living on your desktop.**

PetFam is a desktop pet for Windows and macOS. Adopt a puppy or a kitten, and it moves in *behind your windows*: it walks around on your wallpaper, treats the scenery as its home, naps in a little house you build for it, peeks out from the edge of the window you are working in, and grows up with you day by day.

PetFam is not a screensaver and does not get in your way. Your pets live on the desktop layer, under every app window, and only come to the front when you reach for them.

> Status: early development. Windows 10 21H2+ / Windows 11, and macOS 11+ (new: built and packaged, but not yet tried on a real Mac).

## Why PetFam

- **They live in your wallpaper.** PetFam reads your wallpaper and finds the floor, water, sunny spots, shade, mud and even houses in the picture. Dogs jump into a lake and shake off, cats sunbathe and avoid puddles. Change the wallpaper and they adapt within seconds.
- **Real breeds with real personalities.** 17 dogs and 16 cats, each with its own look, temperament, habits and way of talking: a Shiba that screams when picked up, a Ragdoll that goes limp in your arms, a Corgi who only talks about food, a Husky who talks back, a Siamese who never stops chatting. Each is built like the real thing: a Golden Retriever or Maine Coon stands well above a Chihuahua; Poodles and Bichons are curly, a Maltese's silky coat falls to the floor, Pomeranians and Huskies wear a big ruff, a Husky has its dark cap and white mask, long-haired cats have a mane and britches, a British Shorthair has round jowls, a Bengal has leopard spots, and a Siamese's dark points run down its legs. Faces are breed-true too: a Dachshund's long nose and a Frenchie's pushed-in one, a Golden's broad muzzle and soft hanging ears, a Shiba's small eyes and pale eyebrow spots, a Chihuahua's big round eyes, a Siamese's dark face mask and long wedge-shaped face, and a Maltese with a bow in its topknot.
- **They grow up with you.** Every pet arrives as a two-month-old baby and grows a day older on each day you spend together, through junior and adult to a dignified senior. Body, energy and behaviour change with age.
- **A home of their own.** Build and decorate houses, decide whose house is whose, and watch your pets walk in through the door to sleep or to get out of the rain.
- **The real weather.** When it rains where you live, it rains on your desktop. Cats run for cover, dogs play in the puddles, snow piles up on the roofs.
- **All of your desktops and screens.** Pets travel between Windows virtual desktops, follow you when you switch, or stay home, and stroll from one monitor to the next.
- **Gentle by design.** No chores, no guilt trips: pets never die, and simply being at your computer keeps your bond from fading. Full-screen apps and presentations put them on quiet mode.

## Features

### Life on the desktop
- Pets live between the wallpaper and your windows; when a window covers them, they are simply behind it.
- Hover over a pet and it comes to the front, looks at you and can be petted, picked up and carried.
- **Window peek:** a curious pet walks behind the window you are using and pokes its head out of an edge to look at you.
- Cats hunt a moving mouse pointer: crouch, stalk, wiggle, pounce.
- A life of their own: sudden zoomies across the floor and panting afterwards (young and lively breeds most; cats after dark), dogs chasing their tails until dizzy, nose-down sniffing trails (hounds and foodies love it), cats watching something up in the sky and chattering at it, begging under your pointer when peckish, trotting after your pointer once they trust you, and big yawns when tired.
- Mischief: cats stare you in the eye and knock the food bowl away, climb up and sit on top of the window you are using, and lounge on a house roof; dogs trot up to your pointer and play bow to ask for a game, and bark at a newly opened window (wary breeds most). Both chase butterflies on sunny days, pouncing again and again; it always gets away. Cats knead with purring paws, and dogs give their ears a good scratch with a hind foot.
- Pets play with each other and remember who their friends are and who is boss.

### Care and bond
- Sims-style pie menu on every pet: Pet, Chin scratch, Hug, Play ball, Give a treat, Feed, Towel dry, Sit!, Rest. Actions queue up and the pet does them in order.
- Needs (hunger, energy, fun, affection, cleanliness), mood and a bond that unlocks more as you get closer.
- Throw the ball yourself: grab it with the mouse and flick it; dogs fetch it back.
- Pets get wet and muddy, shake off and dry in the sun.
- **Dress-up:** collars, a bell collar, bandanas, bow ties and scarves; ribbons, flower clips, beanies, straw hats
  and a crown; sweaters, vests and raincoats; round glasses and sunglasses, each in several colours (pet panel →
  👗 Dress up, or tray → My pets → the pet → Dress up). It's all free: four pieces from day one, the rest unlock as
  your bond grows. "Dress for the weather" puts on a raincoat in the rain and a scarf and beanie in the snow, and a
  pet in a raincoat minds the rain much less. Pets react in character: a Maltese or Persian is delighted, a Shiba or
  Husky shakes itself and grumbles.
- **Dress-up mischief and couple looks:** a pet that doesn't like dressing up now and then shakes its hat or glasses
  off onto the floor ("Free at last!"); put it back from the pet panel (🎩 Put it back on), or it is talked into it
  again after ten minutes. A playful dog may find it first, wear it on a victory lap and drop it somewhere else.
  Pets in matching outfits (a **couple look**: "dress them the same" in the dress-up window) grow closer faster and
  now and then come side by side to show it off: "We match!" "Twins!"
- **Your own colours:** give any pet the coat, belly, marking and eye colours you like, from suggested swatches or
  any colour at all (pet panel → 🎨 Change colours, or tray → My pets → the pet → Change colours). Shades of the coat
  follow along, the breed's markings stay, and "Breed colours" puts everything back.

### Growing up
Baby (days 0–14) → Junior (15–45) → Adult (46–365) → Senior (366+). A day counts when you have been at the computer for ten minutes, so pets do not age while the computer is off. Milestones are celebrated with confetti and a party hat.

### Houses
Build up to three houses with a choice of shapes, colours and decorations. Give each one an owner or make it everybody's. Pets walk in through the door, lie in the doorway looking out, and come back out when they wake.

### Weather
Optional real weather from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) for a city you choose: rain with splashes, thunder and lightning, snow that settles and melts, cloud and fog. Pets react in character.

### AI for your pets (optional)
Pets can use an AI you already have: **Claude Code**, **Codex CLI** or **Gemini CLI** (signed in with your own subscription, no key in PetFam), a local model in **Ollama**, or a **Claude API key**. Pick one under *AI for your pets…* in the tray, with a test button. Without one nothing changes.
- **Pets talk to each other** in their own personas: breed temperament, personality, age, mood, outfit and how the two get on. They walk up to each other and take turns in speech bubbles; a warm talk brings them closer.
- **Talk to a pet**: click it → 💬 Talk. It answers in its own voice (also in a bubble on the desktop) and may do what you ask ("go have a rest") if it is willing.
- **Diaries**: every night after 9 pm each pet writes about its day (what it did, who it played with, what you did together) in its own voice. The latest line shows on the pet's panel; *Pet diaries…* in the tray has them all, plus a **weekly story** about everyone.
- **Make things from words**: describe a house ("a cosy cabin with a lantern"), an outfit ("Christmas party"), coat colours ("cream with brown ears") or the pet you'd like to adopt, or show a photo of your own pet, and the AI picks from what PetFam has.
- **The AI reads your wallpaper**: when the wallpaper changes, an AI that can see pictures labels floor, water, sand, sun, shade and sky by itself (once per wallpaper, in the background) and that becomes the habitat; a reading with hardly any floor is ignored. Your own fixes in *Fix the habitat* stay on top, and *Let AI read the wallpaper* there asks again.
- **Pets know where they live**: the AI also describes the scenery in a few words, and the pets treat it as their world. They mention the mountains or the sea in their chats, their diaries and when you talk to them; a pet on its own now and then says something about what it sees. Change the wallpaper and the pets on that screen talk about their new place straight away, and whether they liked the old one better (even if it changed while PetFam was closed). PetFam's own wallpapers come with their scenery built in.
- **MCP tools for your AI apps**: a local server on `127.0.0.1:47800` lets Claude Code, Claude Desktop (through `npx mcp-remote`) and other MCP apps list your pets, feed them, ask them to do things, make them say something, call them over and read their diaries.
- **Coding agent buddy**: when your coding agent is working, finishes, needs you or fails, a pet cheers, celebrates, calls you or comforts you. *Connect Claude Code* in the AI settings adds the MCP server and the hooks (your settings are backed up first).

### Day and night
Day and night follow your computer's clock and the real sunrise and sunset where you are (worked out on your computer for the weather city, no network needed). Dusk and dawn glow warm, nights turn the desktop deep blue with stars on clear nights, the house windows light up, and pets get sleepy after dark.

### Virtual desktops
Each desktop has its own habitat from its own wallpaper. Pets walk off one edge and arrive at the other, follow you when you switch desktops (Follow me), wander on their own (Roam freely) or Stay home, and can be called over from the tray.

### PetFam wallpapers
**Tray → PetFam wallpapers** puts one of PetFam's own pictures on your desktop: **Sunny meadow** (a pond, a sandy
path, a mud puddle, sunny patches and shady trees), **Seaside** (the sea, a beach, a tide pool and a palm's shade) or
**Cozy garden** (a lawn, a fountain, a sandbox, a vegetable patch and a parasol). Each comes with an exact habitat
drawn together with the picture, so pets know every spot without guessing. **Back to my own wallpaper** puts back the
picture you had before (Windows Spotlight, if you used it, has to be switched on again in Settings).

### Habitat editor
The wallpaper analysis gets most pictures right, but not all: a lake read as a beach, a hill pets should be able to
climb. **Tray → Fix the habitat** covers the screen with your wallpaper and colours what PetFam thinks each part is.
Paint over it with a round brush: floor, water, mud, sand, sunny spot, shade, or not floor; "Undo (back to automatic)"
rubs a fix out again. The mouse wheel changes the brush size, Ctrl+Z undoes the last stroke. Save, and the pets use the
new floor straight away. Fixes are kept per wallpaper and screen size, so they come back every time that wallpaper does.

### Multiple monitors
Every monitor is part of the pets' world, each with its own habitat read from the wallpaper on that screen. Now and then a pet walks off the edge of one screen and comes in from the facing edge of the next, at the same height on the floor (Stay home pets keep to their own screen). After a desktop switch, following pets come in on the monitor they were on. Plug in or unplug a monitor and PetFam rebuilds itself to match; pets on a screen that goes away move to the main monitor. Food, houses and new pets go on the main monitor.

### Languages
English by default, Korean optional (tray → Language / 언어). Everything switches, including breed personas and what the pets say.

## Install PetFam (Windows)

You need a Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC. Nothing else: no extra programs, no administrator password.

### 1. Get the installer
Get **`PetFam-Setup-1.0.0.exe`** from whoever shared PetFam with you (by message, mail or a shared drive) and save it,
for example to your **Downloads** folder.

> Your browser may say the file "isn't commonly downloaded". Choose **⋯ → Keep** (Edge) or **Keep** (Chrome).

### 2. Run it
Double-click **`PetFam-Setup-1.0.0.exe`**.

> **"Windows protected your PC"?** This blue box appears for new apps that don't have a paid publisher
> certificate yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

### 3. Click through the setup
1. Pick a language and click **OK**.
2. Leave **"Start PetFam when I sign in to Windows"** ticked, so your pets are there every time you turn on the PC.
   Tick **"Create a desktop shortcut"** if you want an icon on the desktop.
3. Click **Install**, then **Finish**. PetFam starts right away.

It takes under a minute and asks for no administrator rights.

### 4. Adopt your first pet
A window opens: choose **dogs or cats**, pick a **breed**, give your baby a **name** and click **Adopt**.
Your pet appears on the desktop, under your windows.

### Where is PetFam?
PetFam has no big window of its own. It lives in the **tray**, at the bottom right of the screen next to the clock.
If you don't see the PetFam icon (a puppy and a kitten), click the small **^** arrow there.
Click the icon for the menu: your pets, food, houses, weather, language and **Quit**.

To start it again after quitting: **Start menu → PetFam**.

### Update
Run the new `PetFam-Setup-x.y.z.exe` the same way. PetFam closes itself, updates, and your pets are all still there.

### Uninstall
**Settings → Apps → Installed apps → PetFam → ⋯ → Uninstall.**
Your pets are kept (in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PetFam`) in case you install PetFam again. To remove them as well, delete that
folder: press **Win + R**, type `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PetFam`, press Enter, and delete the folder.

### No installer? Use the portable version
`PetFam-1.0.0-portable.zip` holds the same app without an installer. Right-click the zip → **Extract All**, open the
folder and double-click **PetFam.exe**. It doesn't start with Windows by itself and doesn't add a Start menu entry.

## Install PetFam (macOS)

You need a Mac with macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later.

1. Get **`PetFam-1.0.0-macos-arm64.zip`** for a Mac with Apple silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) or
   **`PetFam-1.0.0-macos-x64.zip`** for an older Intel Mac ( → About This Mac tells you which).
2. Double-click the zip to unpack it and drag **PetFam** into your **Applications** folder.
3. The first time, **right-click PetFam → Open**, then **Open** again. (macOS asks this once for apps that are not
   from the App Store yet. If it only offers to cancel, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click
   **Open Anyway**.)
4. Adopt your first pet. PetFam lives in the **menu bar** at the top right of the screen (no Dock icon); click the
   puppy-and-kitten icon for the menu.

To start PetFam when you log in: **System Settings → General → Login Items → +** and pick PetFam.
Pets are on every Space (desktop), and follow you when you switch. Your pets are kept in
`~/Library/Application Support/PetFam`.

### Using PetFam
- **Tray menu:** My pets (where they are, call them over, travel mode), Weather, AI for your pets, pet diaries, pets chat with each other, put out a food bowl, build a house, PetFam wallpapers, fix the habitat, add a pet, language, quit.
- **Mouse:** hover a pet to bring it forward; click for its menu; press and rub to pet; drag to carry. Click a house to decorate it, drag it to move it.
- **Panel:** clicking a pet opens its panel with mood, needs, bond, age and its to-do queue.

## Privacy
- PetFam reads only window positions, the cursor position and idle time, never what is inside your windows.
- The wallpaper is analysed on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
- Apart from the AI you set up, the only network request is the optional weather lookup, which sends the coordinates of the city you picked (no IP lookup, no location permission). Turn it off in the tray.
- AI features (only with an AI set up) send your AI pet information only: names, breeds, personalities, moods, outfits, what they did today, what they said, the weather and time of day, plus what you type to it. A picture is sent only for a photo you pick, and for your wallpaper when *Read each new wallpaper* is on (once per wallpaper; turn it off in AI settings). Never anything from your screen. With Ollama nothing leaves your computer.
- The local server listens on 127.0.0.1 only and refuses requests from web pages. Turn it off in the AI settings.

## For developers

Requirements: Windows 10 21H2 or later and the [.NET 8 SDK](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download).

```
dotnet build PetFam.sln
dotnet test
dotnet run --project src/PetFam.App
```

### Building the installer

```
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File build\build-installer.ps1
```

This publishes PetFam as one self-contained `PetFam.exe` (users need no .NET), compiles `installer\PetFam.iss` with
Inno Setup and zips the portable version. Results are in `artifacts\`: `PetFam-Setup-<version>.exe` and
`PetFam-<version>-portable.zip`. The version comes from `Directory.Build.props` (or `-Version 1.2.0`).
The Inno Setup compiler is taken from `INNO_ISCC`, an installed Inno Setup 6, or downloaded once into `.tools\` from
the `Tools.InnoSetup` NuGet package.

The installer installs per user into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\PetFam` (no administrator prompt), adds a Start menu entry,
optionally a desktop shortcut and start-at-sign-in, and never touches the pets in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PetFam`.
PetFam runs only once at a time: a second copy exits (`PETFAM_MULTI=1` allows more, for developer sheets).

**macOS:** `bash build/build-macos.sh` (on a Mac) publishes PetFam for Apple silicon and Intel, wraps each in a
`PetFam.app` (menu-bar app, no Dock icon, `installer/macos/Info.plist`), signs it ad hoc and zips it. The macOS layer
(`src/PetFam.Platform.MacOS`) talks to the system directly: the backyard is a window just above the desktop picture on
every Space, the foreground window comes from CGWindowList (rectangles only, no Accessibility permission), Spaces
from the window server.

**Automatic builds:** pushing a tag such as `v1.0.0` runs `.github/workflows/release.yml` on GitHub Actions: tests,
the Windows installer and portable zip, and the two macOS zips, attached to a draft release. It can also be started by hand from the Actions tab; the files
are then under the run's artifacts.

The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen warns on first run (see the install steps above). Signing the
exe and the installer with a code-signing certificate removes that warning.

```
src/PetFam.Core              Platform-independent: pets, breeds, needs, bond, growth, habitat, behaviour, weather, saving
src/PetFam.Platform.Windows  Win32: WorkerW desktop layer, click-through, foreground window, virtual desktops
src/PetFam.Platform.MacOS    Stub (planned)
src/PetFam.App               Avalonia app: windows, tray, 3D (SkSL SDF) and vector renderers, weather service
tests/PetFam.Core.Tests      xUnit
build/wallpapers             Draws the PetFam wallpapers and their habitat maps (python -I build/wallpapers/make_wallpapers.py)
docs/PLANNING.md             Product and technical plan
```

- Data: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PetFam\save.json` (saved every minute and on exit), habitat fixes `habitat-edits.json`, log `petfam.log`, snapshots and debug sheets. Data from the old `ZooZoo` folder is copied over on the first run.
- Frame timing: if the animation ever stutters, `petfam.log` records a `Hitch:` line with what took the time.

### Promo video
`promo/` holds a 50-second promo video made with three.js, in a hand-painted animation style: the real PetFam
wallpapers baked into painted backgrounds (brush strokes, pigment and paper), cel-shaded pets, painted clouds and
petals on the wind. It shows the AI reading the wallpaper, a wallpaper change, decorating a house from words, rain
and snow, and growing up. The soundtrack, an original waltz for piano and strings, is synthesised by
`promo/audio.mjs`, so there is nothing to license. It needs Node.js, Chrome (or Edge) and ffmpeg.

```
cd promo
npm install
node render.mjs              # Korean  -> promo/out/petfam-promo-ko.mp4
node render.mjs --lang en    # English -> promo/out/petfam-promo-en.mp4
```

`node render.mjs --serve` plays it live at http://127.0.0.1:5173/ (add `?lang=en` for English), and
`node render.mjs --stills 3,15` saves single moments as PNGs.

### Short film
`promo/film.html` is *The Window Ride*, a 108-second animated short in pure three.js (no video model): the dog and
the cat leave the desktop on an app window and fly through eight worlds (the room at dusk, a sea of clouds at
sunrise, flower hills with windmills, the sea with dolphins, a firefly forest, a storm, snowy mountains under the
aurora, the moon) and come home. 18 shots, about 3,200 code-rendered frames; the score (one theme arranged for
each world) and all sound effects are synthesised by `promo/film-audio.mjs`.

```
node render.mjs --film             # -> promo/out/petfam-film-ko.mp4
node render.mjs --film --lang en   # English title and end card
```

### Homepage
`site/index.html` is the PetFam homepage: one static page (Korean and English), no build step. The characters are
2D: each pet is an SVG built from a few flat shapes and animated with CSS (walking legs, wagging tail, blinking,
ears). In the desktop demo at the top they walk on the real PetFam wallpaper behind draggable app windows; the cat
follows the pointer and a click makes a pet hop and speak. Below it, a bento grid has small live demos (peeking from
behind a window, chatting, a house built from a sentence, weather, growing up, dress-up). The soft moving gradient
behind the headline is a three.js shader, with a CSS gradient as the fallback. Open the file, or host the `site/`
folder anywhere. `node promo/shot-site.mjs` saves full-page screenshots to `promo/out/site`.
